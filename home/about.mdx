Swap Bitcoin trustlessly anywhere in as little as 30 seconds.

Ask Garden for a swap and every [provider](./fundamentals/providers) that can fill it answers at once, including Garden's own protocol. You pick the quote you want.

Garden has processed billions of dollars in volume, powering Bitcoin swaps across apps like Phantom, MetaMask, Lightspark, LI.FI, and many more.

## Garden Protocol

The Garden protocol provides intent-based Bitcoin rails. You express what you want to swap, an open network of solvers competes to fill the order, and settlement happens on-chain via HTLC contracts. Both sides complete or neither does — your funds stay in your control, start to finish.
