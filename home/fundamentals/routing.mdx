title: "How Garden Routes a Swap"
description: "Understand what happens between asking for a swap and choosing how it executes"
---

When you ask Garden for a swap, every [provider](./providers) that supports the pair answers with a quote. You compare what each one offers and pick the one you want, and that provider executes your swap.

Garden is one of those providers. The pages in this section describe the Garden protocol - how a swap settles when you choose Garden. Choose another provider and it settles on that provider's own terms.

## What happens when you swap

<Steps>
  <Step title="You describe the swap">
    The assets you are sending and receiving, and the amount.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Providers answer">
    Every provider that supports the pair returns a quote, with its own rate, fees, and deadline.
  </Step>

  <Step title="You choose">
    Compare the quotes and pick one.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The provider executes">
    Each quote comes with the steps you follow to complete the swap.
  </Step>
</Steps>
