title: "Providers"
description: "Understand who can execute a swap, and how Garden fits among them"

Providers are the systems that can execute a swap. When you ask for a price, every provider that supports the pair answers with its own quote - its own rate, its own fees, and its own deadline, and you choose the one you prefer.

Garden is one of those providers. Choose Garden and your swap settles through the [Garden protocol](./core-concepts/atomic-swaps), with the [solvers](./core-concepts/solvers) and [auctions](./core-concepts/auctions) described in this section. Choose another provider and it settles on that provider's own terms.

## Who are the providers?

| Provider | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Garden** | The Garden protocol, settling through atomic swaps. |
| **Flashnet** | Deposit-based routing across supported chains. |
| **SwapKit** | Aggregated liquidity across its own provider network. |

A provider only answers when it supports the pair you asked for, so the number of quotes changes from one swap to the next.

## What differs between providers?

Each provider decides how it executes, which affects what you see on a quote.

| | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Rate** | How much you receive for what you send. |
| **Fees** | What the provider charges, shown separately on every quote. |
| **Speed** | How long the swap is expected to take. |
| **Deadline** | The time before which the deposit should be sent. |
| **Steps** | What you do to complete the swap. Sending to a deposit address and signing a transaction are both common. |

<Note>
    Deadlines are set by the provider, and some are short. Check the deadline on your quote before you begin.
</Note>
