---
title: "Deadlines"
description: "Understand how long you have to send funds after a quote"
---

Every quote from the [Aggregator API](/developers/api/aggregator) carries `deposit_by`, the time by which the funds must be sent.

This is set by the provider that issued the quote, not by Garden, and it varies from one provider to the next. Some are short. Read `deposit_by` from the quote rather than assuming a duration, and do not start a wallet flow your user cannot finish inside it.
