---
title: "Deadlines"
description: "Understand how long you have to send funds after a quote"
---

Every quote from the [Aggregator API](/developers/api/aggregator) carries `deposit_by`, the time before which the deposit should be sent. A later deposit is still accepted and repriced.

This is set by the provider that issued the quote, and it varies from one provider to the next. Some are short. If the provider sets none, it is 5 minutes after the quote. Read `deposit_by` from the quote rather than assuming a duration, and do not start a wallet flow your user cannot finish inside it.
